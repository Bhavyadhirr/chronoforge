ORATIS-T

2025 · PTSD-Quantification Algorithm


The Beginning

ORATIS-T is a ML-driven neuro-cognitive architecture developed to quantitatively assess PTSD severity using structured behavioral, temporal, and cognitive indicators, particularly the linguistic markers.


Rather than relying on binary diagnostic classification, ORATIS-T models PTSD as a continuous latent state, inferred from multi-modal inputs such as response latency, stimulus sensitivity, emotional regulation markers, and longitudinal behavioral drift. Utilizing the DAIC-WOZ dataset by the University of Southern California, the system integrates temporal modeling techniques to capture non-stationary cognitive patterns associated with trauma progression and recovery.

The Architecture

At its base, the architecture combines:

  • feature-space abstraction of cognitive markers
  • temporal inference mechanisms
  • severity score calibration under uncertainty

The objective, as Aakriti, the Research Lead of A.N.I.M.A. at GENORROW was very thorough with, was not diagnostic replacement, but clinical decision support, offering interpretable severity trajectories that can assist therapists and clinicians in monitoring treatment efficacy over time. The very idea of ORATIS-T emphasizes ethical constraints, explainability, and human-in-the-loop evaluation, ensuring alignment with responsible AI practices in mental health contexts.
