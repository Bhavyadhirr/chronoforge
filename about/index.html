About

I do all that I do because of the 'inkling'.



I've had the predisposition and 've further refined it to approach technology, science, research, and innovation, with not a barometer for how good I am at it, but how inclined I am to it.



The reason why most of my work right now sits at the intersection of quantum computation, Artificial Intelligence, governance-first system design, autonomous security, and physical AI (robotics), is because I'm guided by inkling that has a lot to do with wanting to fathom in how the mind works — natural or artificial; how Intelligence emerges, behaves and should be constrained or optimized.



Much of my focus right now converges on security and robustness in intelligent systems with a growing degree of autonomy and uncertainty. Such applicative areas generally contextualise themselves where failure is extremely costly and ambiguity is often unavoidable.



That marks cryptographic architectures, cognitive modelling, and decision-vectors. I have come to notice that I'm especially drawn towards the areas that pertain to crafting GOVERNANCE for the double-edged tech-work.



A core priority of my philosophy has also emerged out to be the alignment of synthetic advancement with the human embrace. I'm less interested in maximal performance than in understanding WHY a system behaves as it does, and how that behavior scales under, what I call the "uncertainty factor" of life and living.



At a more personal level, I value practices that cultivate patience and tranquility; music, writing, and martial arts, being the quite staple of 'em. I firmly believe that such 'outside' endeavors become modes of engagement with one's relationship with adaptability, timing, optimization under constraint, and unyielding grit.



That's quite a rhetoric, but quite earnestly, this portfolio isn't intended to be a catalogue of finished products or undying success, but more like a reflective dossier spanning time itself. Ideas and work presented here are exploratory by design. One needs to award themselves the freedom to articulate incomplete thought, unpolished assumptions, cognitive revisions.



And yeah, that's pretty much it.
